Promise.allSettled vs Promise.all for parallel LLM calls. Releasy generates 6 launch assets from one GitHub repo with 6 Mistral calls at once. With Promise.all, one failed call rejects the whole batch. With allSettled, the other 5 come back and only the failed one needs a retry.
